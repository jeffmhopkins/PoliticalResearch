# Source map

Write this before deep research. One section per source you expect to use.

For each source record:

- Name and URL
- Tier (T1–T4)
- Who publishes it
- Funding or ownership, with a citation, or "not found (searched: ...)"
- What it is allowed to support (facts, or attributed positions only)

Cover:

1. The election office and the official pamphlet.
2. Finance portals (FEC or the state site).
3. Nonpartisan references you will actually open.
4. For each candidate: the official ballot or elections office for party, then a filing or public voter-registration record, and the endorsement pages you will open (the group's statement or the candidate's site).
5. For a bill: the legislature's bill page, staff analysis, fiscal note, roll calls, lobbyist disclosure, and campaign-finance filings. Florida runs go through `scripts/legislation_lookup.py`.
4. Major organizations on each side of a measure or race, in pairs.

Do not copy a source's "about" page into the guide as fact without a second look.
